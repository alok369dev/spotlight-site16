About Spotlight

EST. FOUNDED AS A LEAN, SYSTEMS-FIRST AGENCY.

What Spotlight is

Spotlight is a media & marketing intelligence agency. It runs digital marketing, PR & coverage monitoring, creative production and influencer coordination through one connected system — automation handles the repetitive pipes, and a human approves every decision that actually matters.

Unlike a traditional agency that staffs up for every repetitive task, Spotlight is built systems-first: off-the-shelf tools by default, custom software only where it creates a lasting edge, and a strict rule that nothing publishes, spends, or sends without a person clicking approve.

How We Work

We automate the pipes. Never the relationships.

$never --pitch --negotiate --auto-approve # relationships stay human
